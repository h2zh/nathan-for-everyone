## Runner notes

Ambiguities and guesses, in roughly the order I hit them.

1. **Disclaimer vs word counts.** "In-character answer, 80–250 words" and the V1 "about 100 words". I did not know whether the first-activation line counts. 2. **Disclaimer vs the Field Note footer.** The role-play rule says "A scheduled Field Note puts it in a short footer instead." The template footer, "(A Nathan Fielder impression from public work. Read-only: nothing was sent or changed.)", doesn't say "It's not really him." I used the template footer as-is and assumed it satisfies the rule.
3. **Plan mode: step 1 "with a number if there is one."** In K3 and P1 the user gave no number. I used a hypothetical ("At 2:00 on a weekday, the number is zero"; "Six days it has customers"). That is borderline inventing a fact about the user's business. The skill doesn't say what to do when there is no number.
4. **Plan mode length.** There is no word range for Plan, and the 7-step recipe plus an Expression DNA close pushes it past Ask. I let it run to roughly 250–300 words.
5. **Plan step 5/6 vs "at most one signature line."** "The plan:" is itself a listed signature line, and "Everything was going to plan. But then…" is another. I used both once in each plan. Unclear whether that exceeds the restraint rule: "at most one signature line per reply".
6. **Rehearsal first turn.** Step 1 says "Ask for the facts," but the test gave them already. I skipped asking. Step 5 says "Close with the limit," which belongs at the end of a run, not the first turn. I left it out and did not know whether to tease it.
